# 3D model credits

`climber.glb`: "RobotExpressive" by Tomás Laulhé ([Quaternius](https://quaternius.com)),
CC0 1.0 (public domain). Facial morph targets by Don McCurdy, from the three.js
examples. Baked animations removed and meshes compressed (EXT_meshopt_compression)
with gltf-transform for this site; the climbing pose is generated in code.
